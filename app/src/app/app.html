LOCAL INGREDIENTS

Let’s talk about Asin tibuok.

A traditional Boholano sea salt, handmade using natural materials and an age-old process.

Asin tibuok is made by soaking coconut husks in seawater for about three months, then drying and burning them into ash. The ash is filtered and leached with seawater to produce a brine, which is poured into specially made clay pots and boiled over a wood fire until it hardens into a solid, dinosaur egg-shaped block of salt.
